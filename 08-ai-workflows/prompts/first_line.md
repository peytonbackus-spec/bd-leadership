# LLM Prompt: Cold Email Opening Line

Write a personalized, casual, professional 1-sentence opening line for a cold outreach email.

Prospect: {{first_name}} {{last_name}}, {{title}} at {{account_name}}
Trigger Signal: {{signal_description}}

Constraints:
- Max 20 words.
- No fluff or overly polite greetings ("Hope this email finds you well").
- Directly connect trigger signal to business impact.
