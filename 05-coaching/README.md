# 05. Coaching & Enablement Framework

## Weekly 1:1 Agenda Template
1. **Pipeline & Metrics Check (10 mins):** Activity pacing, conversion ratios, current forecast.
2. **Game Film Review (20 mins):** Review 1 cold call recording and 1 email thread (Gong / Chorus).
3. **Objection Handling Practice (15 mins):** Live roleplay on top blocker of the week.
4. **Action Items & Commitments (5 mins):** Clear targets for next week.

## Cold Call Scorecard (1-5 Scale)
- **Opener:** Confident tone, upfront value proposition, permission granted.
- **Discovery:** Uncovered key business friction or confirmed tech stack.
- **Objection Handling:** Acknowledged, re-framed, and asked control question.
- **Close:** Definite date and time proposed for AE discovery meeting.
