# 04. Outbound Campaigns & Cadences

## Standard 14-Day Multi-Touch Sequence Map
- **Day 1:** Manual Email (Signal Hook) + Phone Call (Orum) + LinkedIn Connection Request.
- **Day 3:** Phone Call + Email Thread Reply (Value Add / Framework).
- **Day 7:** Phone Call + LinkedIn Soft Touch (Engage with Post).
- **Day 10:** Automated Email (Case Study Proof Point).
- **Day 14:** Breakup Email + Final Phone Attempt.

Before Day 1, every contact passes the [pre-send compliance gate](../12-stack-configuration/outreach-sequence-design.md#pre-send-compliance-gate). Where the gate removes a channel (e.g. no cold email to Germany under UWG §7), drop those steps rather than substituting another channel automatically.
