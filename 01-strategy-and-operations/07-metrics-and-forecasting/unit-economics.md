# Unit Economics & CAC Modeling

> Starting targets (common rules of thumb, unsourced here). Set them from your own finance data.

## Key Equations
- **Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC):** CAC = (Total SDR & AE Fully-Loaded Cost + Software Stack Cost) / New Customers Acquired
- **LTV to CAC Ratio Target:** > 3.0x
- **Payback Period Target:** < 12 months
