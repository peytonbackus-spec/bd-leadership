# Outbound Regulatory Compliance Checklist

## Global Standards
- **CASL (Canada):** Explicit consent or verified implied consent (B2B relevance exemption). Must include unsubscribe mechanism and postal address.
- **GDPR / PECR (UK/EU):** Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA) documented. One-click opt-out in every email.
- **CAN-SPAM (USA):** Clear identification of business, physical mailing address, honor opt-outs within 10 days.
