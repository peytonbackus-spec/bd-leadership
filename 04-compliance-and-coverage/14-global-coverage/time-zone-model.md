# Time-Zone Routing Rules

## Schedule Mapping
- **EST/CST Territories:** Calling windows 8:30 AM EST - 11:30 AM EST and 1:30 PM EST - 4:30 PM EST.
- **PST Territories:** Calling windows 8:30 AM PST - 11:30 AM PST and 1:30 PM PST - 4:30 PM PST.
- **EMEA Territories:** Assigned to dedicated regional reps operating 8:00 AM GMT - 4:00 PM GMT.
