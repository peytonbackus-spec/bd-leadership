# Worked Example: Cold Call Scorecard

A filled-in version of the [Cold Call Scorecard](./README.md#cold-call-scorecard-1-5-scale), showing how one call turns into one coaching priority. **The call, rep, and prospect are fictional.** The example is built to show the method, not to report a real result.

## Call summary
- **Rep:** BDR, month 4 of ramp
- **Prospect:** VP Finance, 900-employee logistics company (Tier 1, active hiring signal for an FP&A manager)
- **Outcome:** No meeting booked. Prospect asked for "something by email."
- **Length:** 3 min 40 s

## Scores

| Category | Score (1-5) | Evidence from the call | What a 5 sounds like |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Opener** | 4 | Named the reason for the call (the FP&A hire) in the first 15 seconds and asked for 30 seconds. Tone was steady. | Same, plus a one-line reason why the hire matters to *them* ("new FP&A leads usually inherit a close process they didn't build"). |
| **Discovery** | 2 | Asked one question ("How's the close going?"), got "fine," and moved straight to pitching. | Two layered questions: "When the new FP&A manager starts, what's the first thing you'll want them to fix?" then "What happens today when that slips?" |
| **Objection handling** | 2 | On "send me something," said "Sure, what's your email?" and ended the call. | Acknowledge, reframe, control question: "Happy to. So I send the right thing, is the bigger issue speed of the close or confidence in the numbers?" |
| **Close** | 3 | Asked "Would next week work?" with no specific time. | Propose a specific slot and an alternative: "Tuesday at 10 or Thursday at 2, your time?" |
| **Total** | **11 / 20** | | |

## Coaching output (what goes into the 1:1)
1. **One priority, not four:** discovery. The objection came *because* there was no pain on the table to anchor a meeting.
2. **Drill (15 min role-play):** the manager plays the VP Finance and answers "fine" to the first question three times. The rep has to get to a second-layer question each time.
3. **Commitment for next week:** on 10 calls, ask at least two discovery questions before any pitch. Self-score those 10 calls on discovery only.
4. **Measure:** discovery scores on next week's sampled calls, and the connect-to-meeting rate over the next 50 connects (one week is too small a sample to judge the rate).

## How to use the scorecard well
- Score from the recording, not from memory, and quote the evidence.
- Pick one category to coach per week. A rep who gets four priorities fixes none.
- Calibrate as a team monthly: everyone scores the same call, then compare. Any category where scores differ by more than 1 point needs a sharper definition.
