# 06. SDR Hiring & Onboarding Framework

## 30-60-90 Day Onboarding Roadmap

### First 30 Days: Foundations & Tooling
- Pass Product & ICP Certification exam (Score > 85%).
- Complete stack training (Salesforce, Outreach, Orum, ZoomInfo).
- Shadow 15 AE discovery calls and 20 SDR cold call blocks.

### Days 31-60: Execution & Ramp
- Execute first full active sequence blocks (50% quota target).
- Conduct weekly game film reviews.
- Achieved target: Minimum 10 SALs booked.

### Days 61-90: Full Quota Acceleration
- Operate at 100% full quota capacity.
- Self-source 80%+ of target pipeline.
