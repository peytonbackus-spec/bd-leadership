# SDR Retention & Team Health

## Career Pathing Architecture
- **SDR I (Months 0-6):** Core outbound execution, mastering tech stack.
- **SDR II (Months 7-12):** Tier 1 enterprise accounts, onboarding buddy for new hires.
- **Senior SDR / Team Lead (Months 13-18):** Advanced deal structuring, lead co-coaching sessions.
- **Promotion Tracks:** Account Executive (AE), Customer Success Manager (CSM), RevOps Analyst, Sales Enablement.
