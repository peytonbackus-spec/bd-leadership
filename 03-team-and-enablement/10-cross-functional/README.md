# 10. Cross-Functional Alignment (SDR - AE - Marketing)

## SLA Matrix
- **Marketing to SDR:** Inbound MQLs routed within 5 minutes; SDR touches within 15 minutes.
- **SDR to AE:** SAL handoff submitted via Salesforce within 2 hours of call completion.
- **AE Feedback Loop:** AE accepts or rejects SAL with detailed disposition reason within 24 hours of scheduled meeting.
