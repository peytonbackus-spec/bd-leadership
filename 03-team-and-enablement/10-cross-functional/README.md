# 10. Cross-Functional Alignment (SDR - AE - Marketing)

For what I've actually run with AEs, Sales Ops, Customer Success and marketing (stage gates, routing, renewal handoffs, MQL recycling, Gong and Clay feedback loops), see the [Cross-Functional Playbook](./cross-functional-playbook.md).

## SLA Matrix

> Proposed starting SLAs, not measured results. The inbound response tiers are defined in the [speed-to-lead contract](../../gtm-prompt-library/contracts/speed-to-lead-sla-contract.md).
- **Marketing to SDR:** Inbound MQLs routed within 5 minutes; SDR first touch within the tiered window (Tier 1: 5 minutes, Tier 2: 30 minutes, Tier 3: next business day).
- **SDR to AE:** Meeting Held handoff submitted via Salesforce within 2 hours of call completion.
- **AE Feedback Loop:** AE accepts or rejects the handoff with detailed disposition reason within 24 hours of scheduled meeting.
