# Case Study: Scaling SDR Org & Outbound Revenue Engine (FP&A SaaS, 3-Year Tenure)

> Outcomes below are self-reported and qualitative. Company details are anonymized, and no figures are claimed.

## Executive Summary
Managed and scaled the Sales Development function at an FP&A SaaS company over a 3-year tenure. Implemented systematic account scoring, signal tracking, and structured execution frameworks.

## Key Outcomes (self-reported)
- **Pipeline Expansion:** Built and managed SDR teams delivering consistent outbound qualified pipeline.
- **Conversion Optimization:** Applied signal-based account tiering (financial leadership changes, FP&A hiring signals) to improve account-to-meeting conversion.
- **Onboarding Efficiency:** Streamlined rep ramp and standardized dial and email cadences to shorten time to first quota.

## Core Initiatives Executed
1. **Tiered ICP Weighting:** Built scoring logic that combines firmographic fit with financial-stack and leadership signals. The [scoring script](../../01-strategy-and-operations/02-icp-and-account-scoring/score_accounts.py) in this repo is a sample-data reconstruction of the approach.
2. **Cadence Governance:** Standardized multi-touch Outreach sequences and daily Orum call-block structures.
3. **AE-SDR Alignment:** Established strict handoff criteria and weekly deal-review cadences with AE leadership.
