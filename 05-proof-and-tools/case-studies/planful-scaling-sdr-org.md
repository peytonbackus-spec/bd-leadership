# Case Study: Scaling SDR Org & Outbound Revenue Engine (Planful)

## Executive Summary
Managed and scaled the Sales Development function at Planful over a 3-year tenure (ending April 2025), implementing systematic account scoring, automated signal tracking, and structured execution frameworks.

## Key Outcomes
- **Pipeline Expansion:** Built and managed high-performing SDR teams delivering predictable outbound qualified pipeline.
- **Conversion Optimization:** Applied signal-based account tiering (financial leadership changes, FP&A hiring signals) to maximize account-to-meeting conversion rates.
- **Onboarding Efficiency:** Streamlined rep ramp cycles and standardized dial/email cadences to accelerate time-to-first-quota.

## Core Initiatives Executed
1. **Tiered ICP Weighting:** Formulated scoring logic combining firmographic fit with real-time financial stack and leadership signals.
2. **Cadence Governance:** Standardized multi-touch Outreach sequences and daily Orum call block structures.
3. **AE-SDR Alignment:** Established strict SAL handoff criteria and weekly deal-review cadences with AE leadership.
