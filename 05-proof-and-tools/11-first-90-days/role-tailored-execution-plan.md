# First 90 Days Execution Blueprint (GTM & BD Leadership)

A structured onboarding and execution roadmap designed to transition from rapid operational diagnosis to pipeline acceleration and team scaling.

---

## Days 1–30: Audit, Alignment & Baseline Discovery

* **Tech Stack & Pipeline Audit:**
  * Map current tool configurations, API integrations, and field sync rules (Salesforce, Outreach, Dialers, Clay).
  * Review conversion rates across each stage (MQL -> SAL -> SAO -> Closed Won).
* **Cross-Functional Listening Tour:**
  * Interview AE, Sales Engineering, Product, and Marketing leaders to identify friction points in SDR lead handoffs.
  * Conduct shadow sessions on 15+ live BDR calls and outbound call blocks.
* **Deliverable:** Produce a 30-Day GTM Diagnostic & Stack Efficiency Report.

---

## Days 31–60: Standardization, Prompting & Workflow Optimization

* **Data & Signal Integration:**
  * Implement automated account scoring and signal decay models to prioritize target accounts.
  * Refine Outreach sequence templates, call scripts, and objection-handling battlecards.
* **Enablement & Coaching:**
  * Roll out weekly call-review scorecard framework and 1:1 coaching cadences.
  * Establish clear SLAs for SDR-to-AE opportunity qualification (MEDDPICC / SPICED).
* **Deliverable:** Optimized sequence rollout and standardized SDR playbook deployment.

---

## Days 61–90: Scale, Automate & Measure Lift

* **AI & Workflow Automation:**
  * Deploy automated LLM account brief generation for high-tier accounts prior to rep phone blocks.
  * Establish automated prompt evaluation criteria to prevent low-quality rep messaging.
* **Capacity & Pipeline Growth:**
  * Finalize capacity model forecasts and ramp targets for upcoming quarters.
  * Conduct 90-day progress review with executive leadership outlining ROI lift and conversion improvements.
* **Deliverable:** Enterprise GTM Operating Framework & Quarter-over-Quarter Growth Roadmap.
