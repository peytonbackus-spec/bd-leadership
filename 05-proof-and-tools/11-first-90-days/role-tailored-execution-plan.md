# First 90 Days Execution Blueprint
**Role:** Manager, Business Development (BDR Leadership)  
**Focus:** Building, coaching, and scaling high-performing outbound revenue development teams.

---

## Executive Summary

The primary objective of BDR leadership is driving predictable, repeatable pipeline growth while building a high-trust, high-accountability coaching environment. Technology and AI automation serve as leverage for reps—maximizing phone time, research speed, and conversion rates—never as a substitute for core sales fundamentals.

---

## Days 1–30: People, Phones & Baseline Diagnostics

### 1. People & Culture First (Week 1)
* **1:1 Connections:** Conduct individual 1:1 sessions with every BDR. Map personal motivators, career ambitions, current pain points, and tool friction.
* **Live Shadowing & Phone Time:** Spend active hours on phone blocks in Orum alongside reps. Shadow live calls, evaluate objection-handling patterns, and observe real-time workflow bottlenecks.
* **Cross-Functional Alignment:** Meet with AE managers, RevOps, and Marketing leaders to review current BDR-to-AE handoff satisfaction and lead quality.

### 2. Operational & Pipeline Diagnostic (Weeks 2–4)
* **Conversion Audit:** Evaluate conversion rates across the core outbound funnel:
  $$\text{Contacts Sourced} \rightarrow \text{Connects (Orum)} \rightarrow \text{Meeting Set} \rightarrow \text{Meeting Held} \rightarrow \text{SQO}$$
* **Coaching Baseline:** Review call recordings in Gong/Chorus to establish baseline team scoring across opening hooks, discovery questions, and objection handling.
* **Tooling Audit:** Verify Salesforce $\leftrightarrow$ Outreach $\leftrightarrow$ Orum field mappings and clean up rep task friction.

---

## Days 31–60: Coaching Rigor, Execution & Optimization

### 1. Structured Coaching Cadence
* **Weekly Game Film Sessions:** Launch team call review sessions analyzing top 10% call clips and turning lost calls into coaching moments.
* **Role-Play Framework:** Implement 30-minute weekly objection-handling simulations covering standard buyer pushbacks.
* **AE Handoff Alignment:** Enforce light BDR-to-AE qualification SLAs (`Meeting Held` to `SQO` criteria) so reps are rewarded for high-intent pipeline, not junk meetings.

### 2. Workflow & AI System Support
* **Targeting Alignment:** Deploy `Account_Tier__c` rules (`Tier A`, `Tier B`, `Tier C`) to ensure top accounts receive high-touch multi-channel sequences.
* **Research Automation:** Introduce dry-run AI account briefs to reduce pre-call research time from 15 minutes down to 2 minutes per account.

---

## Days 61–90: Scale, Capacity Planning & Predictability

### 1. Performance & Ramp Management
* **Capacity & Quota Attainment:** Review full team ramp progress against meeting targets (`Set`, `Held`, `SQO`).
* **Career Pathing:** Establish clear promotion criteria from BDR to Senior BDR and AE pipeline readiness.

### 2. Scalable BDR Playbook
* **Outbound Playbook Publishing:** Finalize team playbook covering cold call structures, email opening hooks, LinkedIn outreach patterns, and Orum execution blocks.
* **Predictable Pipeline Engine:** Present quarterly performance analysis and revenue forecast to sales leadership.
