# 11. First 90 Days Leadership Blueprint

## Days 1-30: Audit & System Discovery
- Conduct 1:1 interviews with all SDRs, AEs, and Marketing partners.
- Audit data health in Salesforce and sequence efficiency in Outreach.
- Benchmark current funnel conversion baseline.

## Days 31-60: Infrastructure & Process Standardization
- Implement account scoring scripts and automated lead routing.
- Launch standardized call scorecards and weekly game film sessions.
- Deploy AI account brief generation tools.

## Days 61-90: Scale & Optimization
- Execute headcount capacity models for upcoming quarters.
- Publish formal RoE and handoff SLA documentation.
- Deliver initial pipeline lift performance report to executive leadership.
